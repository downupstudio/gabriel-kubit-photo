Nowe Rokicie is a former village incorporated into Łódź in stages, with parts annexed in 1906, 1915, and definitively in 1946. It lies in the Górna district and, since 2005, has been an area of the Municipal Information System. Large-scale housing construction began after World War II. The estate was built on 43 hectares with an intended housing density of 45 m² per unit, and its layout concentrated commercial and cultural functions along a north-south axis. By the early 1970s, industrial building technology was used for the final blocks. Four 13-story towers completed around 1962 were the first complex of their kind in the city.
