The control room at EC-2 is the only interior in the plant still listed in the heritage register. Operators worked there for decades, monitoring the boilers and turbines and making decisions that affected the city. The room had hundreds of buttons, indicators and levers. In 2023 its walls were still largely intact; since then the wall panels have been cut out for scrap. Only the main control desk survives, and it is in poor condition. The control room is one of the best records of how EC-2 operated and also one of the most heavily damaged parts of the complex.
