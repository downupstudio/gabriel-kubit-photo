That structure is an elevated pipe trestle — the kind of steel lattice framework built to carry EC-2's heavy utility lines, including hot water and high-pressure steam, out of the boiler house. It had to be routed high up in the air because the plant's dedicated rail siding ran right underneath it. Coal trains coming off the Łódź Kaliska and Chojny lines used those tracks to reach the wagon tippers and coal yards, so the steam and heating pipes couldn't block their path. After the plant shut down in 2015, the rails were torn up, leaving just the steel frame along the fence line with its shutoff valves and grated catwalk still in place. The caged maintenance ladder is completely covered in overgrowth now, which is exactly what Tadeusz and I climbed to get up on the platform next to the pipes.
