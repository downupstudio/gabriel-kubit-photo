The boiler hall at EC-2 held nine boilers. Four of them were dismantled before the plant was shut down, leaving boilers 5 through 9 in the hall. After the site was bought, the upper floor was demolished, and boiler K-5 was partially dismantled along with it. Boilers K-6, K-7 and K-8 survived in good condition, with only the metal components cut out for scrap missing. Boiler K-9 was dismantled from the top, so it now looks as if it has been cut in half. The boiler hall is one of the best-preserved parts of the entire complex.
